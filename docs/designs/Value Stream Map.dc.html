READING THE MAP

Eight symbols, four rules

Rules: every loop starts at a check. A check either sends work back to an upstream do (arc above the line) or hands it to its own fix (arrows beside it). Fix states never sit on the main line. Gates take no time.

implementDO
Do

Changes the work. Its first pass counts as value-add (green). When a check sends work back, the repeat visit counts as rework.

smokeCHECK
Check

Inspects the work. Needed, but not value-add (slate). The bar along the bottom shows how often work passes on the first try. VSM calls this %C&A.

smoke_fixFIX
Fix

Runs only because a check failed. Drawn dashed, beside its check and off the main line. Every minute it spends is rework.

Gate

Routes the work and takes no time. A loop back through a gate, like “next task”, is planned iteration: drawn dashed gray, not counted as waste.

Sent back

A check returning work to an upstream do. Drawn above the line, and thickness shows laps. Nested arcs make the worst offender obvious.

×22
Fix loop

Fail goes out to the fix, and the fixed work comes back for a re-check. The count sits on the arrows. Re-check time lands on the check as rework.

Fork / join

Branches run at the same time. Lead time follows the slowest branch (the critical path). The others finish early and wait at the join. That wait is slack, not queue.

Queue & timeline

The triangle shows runs queued in front of a state. On the stepped timeline, rises are waiting and valleys are working, colored by the kind of state.

FAN-OUTS

Two kinds, one treatment

Both kinds get fork and join bars, a timeline that follows the slowest branch, and a work total that counts every branch. They differ in whether the branches are named nodes or copies of one node.
