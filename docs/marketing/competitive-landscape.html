The frame everyone uses

Dan Shapiro’s five levels, and the missing middle

Shapiro’s January 2026 post gave the industry its vocabulary. The debate since then has been a binary: review every diff (level 3) or turn off the lights (level 5). Nobody sells a deliberate climb to level 4, where you write specs and approve at gates, and the factory runs in between.

Traction

Money flows to factories; attention flows to free methods

The funded players sell autonomy to enterprises. The biggest open-source followings belong to methods and skill packs, not to boards.

Customer pain

The scarce resource is attention, not code

Usage is surging while trust lags. Developers feel faster than they are, can’t tell which agent is waiting on them, and are building DIY phone-approval hacks to cope.

They want two things that pull against each other: autonomy (fire off 20 tasks and sleep) and a leash (60% block agents from unapproved changes, Stack Overflow, May 2026). Explicit gates on a board, answerable from a phone, give them both.

Whose turn is it

Who holds the card, human or agent, as a first-class property across a whole flow. Superset’s “waiting on you” status is the nearest phrase anyone uses, and it’s a status light, not a position.

Only what needs you

Frame mobile and notifications around the human’s attention (“your turn on 3 cards”), not control from a phone.

The governed middle

Level 4 on purpose: between “your life is diffs” and the dark factory.

Kaizen of the factory

Flow metrics, value-stream maps and WIP limits in front of the review gate. Practitioners prescribe it; no board offers it.

A factory you can see

The board is the factory floor, running locally on your own Claude Code. Not a $1k-a-day black box.

Bring your method

Relay is where Spec Kit or superpowers-style skills actually run, with gates and visibility.

Open ground, not proven ground
  • HumanLayer is funded, gates by phase and already uses factory language. It could claim the baton quickly.
  • Small open-source projects (kandev, Fusion, Archon) show the idea occurs to others.
  • The baton’s appeal hasn’t been tested with users yet.
Business model

Free tools don’t earn from usage; teaching can

Two 2026 failures show the trap. The pattern that works is a paid offer next to the free tool, reached from inside it, with a “bring it to work” step at the top.

Cautionary tale 1

Vibe Kanban: loved, free, shut down

2025Defines the category: worktree per card, review column. “Get 10X more out of Claude Code.”
peak28.3k stars, a claimed 30,000 active users and 100,000 PRs.
early 2026HN users notice it “stopped improving and still has bugs.”
Apr 10, 2026Sunset announced. Reportedly it “couldn’t monetize a mostly-free tool.” Now community-maintained.

Those ~30k users are looking for a new home right now. The sunset details come from secondary sources.

Cautionary tale 2

Tailwind: usage up, revenue gone

AI agents answered questions without anyone visiting the docs, and the docs were where people found the paid product. Relay’s users talk to Claude Code, which reads READMEs for them. A paid offer that lives only on a docs page will go unseen.

A possible ladder for Relay hypothesis, untested

No conversion benchmarks were found for free tool → cohort, or individual → team, in AI-coding education.

Step 1Free local board$0

The proof and the lead generator. Must be excellent.

Step 2Free email course or starter flows$0 · email

Offered from inside the runner’s first-run output and empty board states.

Step 3Self-paced courselow ticket

The method, named and codified.

Step 4Live cohort~$800–1,500

For the after-hours builder who wants to level up.

Step 5Team workshop + certified champions$4k+

“Bring it to work.” Triggered by a company email domain or GitHub org, like Tailscale’s.

Analogues: Tailscale (free personal plan, a work domain starts a business trial, “bring Tailscale to work” rewards); Obsidian (free for work, earns on Sync); Kanban University (certifies trainers who resell the method). The method, the baton and its gates, is what you’d certify, not the software.
