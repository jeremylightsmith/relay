Weeknight lemon pasta

25 min · Serves 4 · Easy

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 lemons, zested
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 40g parmesan

Method

  1. 1Boil the pasta in well-salted water.
  2. 2Warm garlic in olive oil; add zest and juice.
  3. 3Toss with pasta, parmesan and basil.
