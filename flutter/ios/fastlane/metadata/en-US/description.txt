Hamaca: Hammock Driven Development.

Hamaca is a kanban board for passing the baton between people and AI agents. Your agents become a factory you can see at a glance, and every card shows whose move it is: yours, or an agent's.

Your phone lights up only when a decision is yours, and what you approve is design docs, mockups and screenshots. Ship more ideas than you have hours, from anywhere.

- Needs you: one list of every card waiting on you, across your boards.
- The board: see what each agent is working on and what's blocked on a human.
- Review: look at the mockups and screenshots, then approve to move the card forward, or send it back with a note.
- Answer: when an agent has a question, answer it in a few taps and hand the baton back.

Sign in with the account you use for your Hamaca board at hamaca.app.
